- Mobile drawer includes the official logo mark for unified brand recognition.

### Hero Section
- **Visual Centerpiece**: `IMG_1334.JPG.jpeg` (Regal Rajasthani Bride) as the high-impact visual element.
- **Composition**: Placed with editorial side alignment so the bride's face, nath, and polki necklace remain 100% unobscured by headline text and CTA buttons.
